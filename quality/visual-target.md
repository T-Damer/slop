# Visual target

User-supplied references are direction and quality bars, not sources of reusable proprietary assets.

## Shared qualities

- Warm, bright, stylized 3D with coherent materials and readable directional light.
- Strong silhouettes: characters, cars, pumps, junk, roads, curbs, vegetation, and buildings remain recognizable at mobile scale.
- Layered environments with authored-looking variation rather than uniform primitive repetition.
- Clear separation between gameplay objects and decoration.
- Restrained game UI. Avoid dashboard, landing-page, glassmorphism, oversized title cards, and generic AI-demo styling.
- Feedback is physical and local to the action: character motion, object motion, particles, rewards, sound/haptics hooks, and camera response.
- Hub artwork is lightweight and original; it suggests each game without reproducing third-party icons or key art.

## Parking Jam

- The parking interaction area, vehicle exit routes, pickup bays, and passenger queue remain unobstructed.
- Review city boot, beach boot, blocked move, passenger boarding, pickup jam, completion, small phone, and tablet landscape.

## Character-driven tycoon worlds

- Use a readable top-down/isometric camera with the player near the visual center and interaction props separated from decoration.
- An available automatic interaction shows a floating local icon and visible hold progress.
- Character poses communicate walking, hammering/building, fueling, talking, and collecting without relying on text alone.
- The weak-device profile may reduce shadows and decoration, but not interaction readability or the core route through the scene.
- Review hub card, yard boot, movement, interaction-in-progress, junk completion, pump construction, fueling, payment, small phone, and desktop.

## Pocket Club

- The table sits in a warm billiard-room composition rather than on a generic app background.
- Ball lighting stays fixed to the room while stripes, number patches, and cue-ball marks roll according to travel direction and distance.
- The left cue/power meter, right direction meter, top player ball slots, spin control, shot action, and sound state remain immediately visible.
- Collision feedback combines short spatially local flashes with distinct cue, ball, cushion, and pocket sounds.
- Review initial rack, high-power break in motion, settled break, potted-ball HUD, small phone, and desktop.

## Rejection criteria

Reject a technically working result when decoration hides gameplay, colors do not match across world and HUD, controls overlap, touch targets are too small, generated assets have inconsistent scale or pivots, interaction progress is unclear, or the composition looks like an AI-generated dashboard rather than a game.
