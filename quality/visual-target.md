# Visual target

The user-supplied reference image is a direction and quality bar, not a source of reusable proprietary assets.

## Required qualities

- Warm, bright, stylized 3D with coherent materials and natural-looking directional light.
- Strong silhouettes: cars, people, roads, curbs, vegetation, and buildings remain recognizable at mobile scale.
- Layered environment with authored-looking variation rather than uniform primitive repetition.
- Clear separation between gameplay objects and decoration.
- The parking interaction area, vehicle exit routes, pickup bays, and passenger queue remain unobstructed.
- Restrained game UI. Avoid dashboard, landing-page, glassmorphism, oversized title cards, and generic AI-demo styling.
- Feedback is physical and local to the action: crowd motion, vehicle motion, particles, score, sound/haptics hooks, and camera response.

## Review views

Every visual change should be reviewed at minimum in:

- city boot;
- beach boot;
- blocked move;
- passenger-group boarding;
- pickup jam;
- completion;
- small phone and tablet landscape.

## Rejection criteria

Reject a technically working result when decoration hides gameplay, colors do not match across world and HUD, controls overlap, touch targets are too small, generated assets have inconsistent scale/pivots, or the composition looks like an AI-generated dashboard rather than a game.
